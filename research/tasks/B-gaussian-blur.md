## Current model (likely wrong)
Nine USC programs (`AGX_USC_BLUR_1..9`) are treated as one blur: a fixed 6-tap-pair kernel (sigma 3.5, "narrow_blur" from QuartzCore) applied **horizontally and vertically in the same draw** (`agx_blur_texture`), for both textured blurs and the untextured stride-12 canvas-backdrop blur. CoreAnimation normally renders a blur as separate one-dimensional passes (horizontal then vertical, each with its own program and offsets), often on a downsampled copy (for example 608x64 or 640x96 targets), and iOS 7 backdrops (status bar, navigation bars, dock, Control/Notification Center, the slider label glow) add saturation and tint afterwards. So the replay may blur twice, blur in the wrong direction, or use the wrong radius.
